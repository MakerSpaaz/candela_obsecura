They continue to try to hide and stay out of his reaching grasp, as Arlo removes one glove (revealing a scarred, blackened hand) and uses her Let Them In ability to learn something shadow-like, a dark entity, was freed in the explosion from its captivity in a carved capsule.

Arlo quickly lights a small fire in a metal bowl, drawing the spectral Donald toward its warmth and allowing them to escape. Howard sends a message to Alexandra telling her what they've learned.
