  Edgar Lycoris is "a skilled if stern surgeon whose operating techniques were honed in the halls of the Grand Halen Hospital."

  Edgar's appearance is unexceptional in every way, and he tends therefore to blend into a crowd. He has small, round glasses and his clothes appear to have been selected by someone else. He appears not to have slept well. He has a large scar on his body, which he keeps hidden under his clothing.

  Edgar's purpose in life has always been to help people, and he sometimes has a difficult time reconciling that with the actions he must take (or fails to take) as a Candela operative.

  Eighteen years ago in 1889, Edgar watched his sister Elizabeth "blow away like dust" in Regent Park, apparently struck by the same kind of force as the Circle later encountered in the battle in the Cemetery from a weapon manufactured by Malcolm Trills' family. She attempted to hold onto Edgar as this happened, leaving burn scars in the shape of her hands on his arm and neck. The War began five years later, in 1894.

  Edgar works at the Grand Halen Hospital. He is a top tier surgeon.
