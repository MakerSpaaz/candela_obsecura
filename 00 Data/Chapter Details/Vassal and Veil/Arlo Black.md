Description: |-
  Arlo lives with her parents in Newfaire. The family is wealthy enough to have servants, including Mr Tellbrook. She was a student of Professor Margrove's, and they have worked together on at least one prior assignment for Candela Obscura.

  Arlo was engaged at some point to a young man named Eddie Easton, who was interested in Oldfaire relics and may have done "some bad things". One day Arlo came to see him and found him being pulled through some sort of portal by a strange gray-skinned creature. In her attempt to save him, her arm went through, turning it cracked and gray and causing her to wear a glove permanently on it.

  In addition to Eddie himself, his entire family and staff also disappeared at the same time. Arlo has heard rumors that it was connected somehow with the clandestine procurers and dealers in Oldfairen artifacts known as the Red Hand.

  Arlo has dark hair and eyes, and is well-dressed befitting a young woman at the turn of the century. She generally wears one glove, concealing a hand that has cracked gray skin and black nails. Along her forearm are deep scratch marks that look old, but not healed.

  Professor Margrove described her as a diligent student, "astute and keen to parse out the details of a person." She grew up in upper class society, with "a certain path set in front of her, but when she was confronted by a devastating paranormal event, she became quite a different person. Because of what happened, she basically lives half in a ghostly realm and half out, often distracted with the activity she sees that others are unaware of".
