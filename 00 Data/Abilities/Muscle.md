>> <font color="#66ff00">**Behind Me**</font>
>> Spend 1 Nerve to choose an ally in the same scene who is about to take a mark from a phenomenon, then describe what you do that allows you to take the mark instead.
>
>> <font color="#66ff00">**Adrenaline Rush**</font>
>> For each mark you take, you may immediately refresh a drive point of your choice.
>
>> <font color="#66ff00"> **Endurance**</font>
>> When you take enough marks to become incapacitated, instead, roll a number of d6 equal to your current Nerve resistance. On a 6, you aren’t incapacitated and don’t take a scar.
