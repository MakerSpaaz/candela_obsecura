>> <font color="#66ff00">**Steel Mind**</font>
>> Once per assignment, when you should take a Brain mark, you may instead burn 1 Intuition resistance to soak it.
>
>> <font color="#66ff00">**University Resources**</font>
>> Your university has alumni all over the world. Once per session, describe a person you know from your tenure as a professor, and ask the GM where they can be found locally.
>
>> <font color="#66ff00">**Learn from My Mistakes**</font>
>> Any time you get a result of 3 or less on a roll, describe what lesson you learned from your failure, and refresh 1 drive point of your choice.
>
>> <font color="#66ff00">**Better Part of Valor**</font>
>> When making a Control or Move roll to flee danger, gild a die. On this roll, the first Nerve you spend is worth +2d instead of +1d.
>
>> <font color="#66ff00">**Verbose**</font>
>> When you make a speech or hold a conversation to assist an ally, the die you give them is gilded.
>
>> <font color="#66ff00"> **Chemical Concoction**</font>
>> You know how to mix chemicals together to achieve particular effects. When you take Laboratory Equipment as gear, you may spend a few minutes concocting a mixture that is: acidic, explosive, flammable, loud, sleep-inducing, sticky, or toxic.
