Members of this organization consider themselves great curators of magick and will advertise this detail when the need arises. Trafficking in the illegal and immoral distribution of magickal artifacts, they always seem to appear in the right place at the right time.

Red Hand members appreciate good taste and clever deals. They have a network of thieves and merchants spanning the globe. After centuries of trafficking magickal phenomena, there is no illicit request they will not oblige.

The Red Hand is a faction that, like [Candela Obscura](https://criticalrole.miraheze.org/wiki/Candela_Obscura_(organization) "Candela Obscura (organization)"), is interested in art and artifacts from the Oldfairen civilization. However, unlike Candela Obscura, which seeks to learn more about Oldfaire and protect the population from magickal threats, The Red Hand wants these artifacts for their power and for the purposes of trade and collection.[[1]](https://criticalrole.miraheze.org/wiki/Red_Hand#cite_note-COx029470-1)

Both the Alizarin Gallery and "Corsen's Curios from around the World and Beyond" shop of [Newfaire](https://criticalrole.miraheze.org/wiki/Newfaire "Newfaire") are owned and managed by the Red Hand, serving as the facade for the black markets of magick artifacts sold by the organization.[[2]](https://criticalrole.miraheze.org/wiki/Red_Hand#cite_note-CandelaObscuraCoreRulebook82-2)

Above all, the Red Hand seeks power and profit. Members of this organization commonly wear gloves. Those who dobusiness with them must ensure they remove their gloves
when shaking on a deal—the Red Hand does not consider a gloved handshake binding.
